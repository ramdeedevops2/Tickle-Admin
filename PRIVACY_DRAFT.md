## What we collect

### Your account

- **Name**, shown on your profile
- **Date of birth**, used to confirm you are 18 or older and to show your age
- **Gender** and **who you are interested in**, used to decide whose profile you see
- **Photos**, **profile video**, **voice prompts** and **bio**, all shown on your profile
- Optional profile details you choose to fill in, such as work, education, languages and interests

### How you sign in

You sign in with **Google**, **Apple**, or your **phone number** with a one-time code. We receive an account identifier and, depending on the method, your email address or phone number. We never receive or store your password for any of these services.

### Location

Location is central to how Gogter works, so this deserves saying plainly.

- We use your location to show you people nearby and to show how far away they are
- With your permission, we also collect location **in the background** to power **Paths Crossed**, which tells you when you and somebody else have been in the same place
- **Your exact location is never shown to another person.** Other members see an approximate distance, never coordinates and never an address
- Background location can be turned off at any time in your phone's settings, and in the app under Settings. Paths Crossed stops working; nothing else does

### Camera and microphone

We ask for these only when you use a feature that needs them:

- **Profile video** and **photos** — recorded or chosen by you, shown on your profile
- **Face verification** — a short selfie capture used to confirm a real person is behind the account. It is compared against your profile photos and is not shown publicly
- **Voice prompts** — short recordings shown on your profile
- **Voice messages** in chat, and **Moments** you post

We do not access your camera or microphone at any other time.

### What you send other people

Messages, photos, voice notes and reactions exchanged with people you have matched with are stored so the conversation works across your devices. Reported content may be retained and reviewed by our moderation team so we can act on it.

### Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by **Apple**. Payment happens entirely inside Apple's system, and **we never see your card details**. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock what you paid for.

### Technical information

We collect a device identifier and basic sign-in records — when and from what device — so we can secure your account and show you your own recent activity.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your personal data
- We do not show your exact location to other members
- We do not use your photos, video or voice to train third-party models
- We do not post anything on your behalf

## Where it is stored

Your data is stored in **Supabase**, which hosts our database and file storage on managed cloud infrastructure. Supabase acts as our processor and stores data on our behalf under contract.

Depending on the region your data is hosted in, it may be transferred outside your country. Where that happens, it is done under appropriate safeguards.

## How long we keep it

- **While your account is open** — for as long as you use Gogter
- **After you delete your account** — your profile stops being visible immediately, and the data is permanently erased after a short grace period
- **Messages** — a message you sent stays visible to the person you sent it to, as it would in any conversation
- **Records we must keep** — a limited amount of information relating to safety reports, payments and legal obligations is kept longer where the law requires it

## Deleting your account

Open the app and go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**.

Deletion is **scheduled, not instant**. There is a grace period before anything is permanently erased, and **signing back in during that window cancels it** — a decision made at 2am should be reversible in the morning. After the grace period ends, your account and its content are permanently deleted.

## Children

Gogter is **strictly for people aged 18 and over**. We ask for your date of birth at signup and refuse accounts below 18. If we learn that somebody under 18 has created an account, we delete it.
